# Third-party art in this folder

`room-tiles.png`, `celeste-sheet.png`, `fable-sheet.png`, `luc-sheet.png` — built from
**LimeZu, "Modern Interiors" (complete v4.1.4)**, https://limezu.itch.io/moderninteriors

Licence: commercial and non-commercial use allowed, editing allowed, **credit required**
(limezu.itch.io). Can't resell or redistribute the raw asset.

Characters are composed from the pack's generator layers by `tools/compose_character.py`.
Pack unpacked at `/mnt/playground/Playground_V2/tilesets/modern-interiors-full/`.

`lake-tiles.png`, and every outdoor prop in `objects.png` — built from
**LimeZu, "Modern Exteriors"**, https://limezu.itch.io/modernexteriors

Same licence, same required credit. Cut by `tools/cut_lake_tiles.py` and the `single`
entries in `tools/cut_objects.py`. Pack unpacked at
`/mnt/playground/Playground_V2/tilesets/modern-exteriors-full/`.

`arceus-sheet.png` — cut by `tools/tsr_extract.py` from a Spriters Resource rip of
Pokémon HeartGold/SoulSilver. Nintendo's pixels: reference and study, not shippable.

`wanderer-*.png` — ours.
